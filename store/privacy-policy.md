# YouTube Mixer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

YouTube Mixer is a browser extension that adds volume, mute, and fade controls
to YouTube video tabs.

## What the extension accesses

To do its job the extension reads, only from your open YouTube tabs:

- the video title and playback state (volume, muted, playing or paused),
- the video thumbnail, shown in the toolbar panel.

This information is used only while the extension is running, to display and
control your tabs. It stays on your computer.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect, store, or record any personal information.
- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- It does not sell or share any data.

## Permissions

The extension requests access to `www.youtube.com` only. It does not have access
to any other website.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the Chrome Web
Store listing.
